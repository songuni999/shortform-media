Shortform Studio

A private publishing tool for the channel owner's own short-form videos.

HomePrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

Uses YouTube API Services

Shortform Studio is an internal tool operated by one independent creator. It uploads short vertical videos that the creator produced themselves (an original illustrated fiction series) to their own YouTube channel, sets titles, descriptions and the "altered or synthetic content" disclosure, and schedules publishing.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0) and authorizes access to their own channel. No other users' data is accessed.

What the tool does

  • Uploads the owner's own videos with videos.insert (scope: youtube.upload).
  • Reads the owner's channel name and the statistics of their own videos (scope: youtube.readonly) to check that publishing succeeded.
  • Updates the privacy status / scheduled publish time of the owner's own videos.
