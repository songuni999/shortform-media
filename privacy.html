This application uses YouTube API Services.

By using Shortform Studio you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube API Services are provided by Google; the Google Privacy Policy applies to data processed by Google.

TikTok — This application also uses the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit to post the operator's own videos to their own TikTok account.

  • TikTok access tokens and the account's open ID, display name and avatar (scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish) are stored only on the operator's local computer and never shared with any third party.
  • No data about other TikTok users, viewers or commenters is accessed or stored.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Stored tokens are deleted within 7 days of revocation.
  • The TikTok Terms of Service and TikTok Privacy Policy apply to data processed by TikTok.

1. Who uses this tool

Shortform Studio is an internal tool used only by its operator (the channel owner) to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information accessed, collected and stored

  • Google OAuth tokens for the owner's own account (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly). Stored only on the owner's local computer, never sent to any third party.
  • The owner's channel name and the IDs, privacy status and public statistics (views, likes) of videos they uploaded with this tool, used to confirm uploads and track performance.
  • The tool does not access, collect or store any data about other YouTube users, viewers or commenters. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.

3. How the information is used

Solely to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to check that they were published correctly. Information is not sold, shared or disclosed to any third party.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Video statistics are kept for at most 30 days and refreshed or deleted after that, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • OAuth tokens are deleted from the local computer when access is revoked or the tool is no longer used.
  • You can revoke this application's access at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation all stored data related to that account is deleted within 7 days.
